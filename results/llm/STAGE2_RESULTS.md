## Set-up

- **Agents:** each agent's update is its endpoint's fitted response function. That is the A3 logit with
  k^-α scaling, plus a first-contact voter step where it beat A3 held-out by ≥ 2% (9 of 54 arms, all
  neutral framing), inertia γ and the content field h_C. Letter and order fields are re-randomised on every
  call (the Stage 3 protocol).
- **Gate-failed endpoints** (Gemma-26B/31B, Mistral Large) are included with explicit per-call jitter. It
  reproduces the jitter-marginal fits within chance (5 of 96 contrasts at |z| > 2).
- **Controls:**
  - the naive Ising reading (Stage 1 additive β only);
  - fields-only (same γ and fields, no social coupling).
- **Validation:** with Ising agents the simulator reproduces the HANDOFF §3 Viana–Bray results.
- **Graphs:** random-regular d = 4 (primary; inside the fitted k ≤ 6) and ER c = 4; ρ ∈ {0, 0.25, 0.5};
  ε ∈ {0, 0.2, 0.5, 1}.
- **Runs:** Stage-3-matched (N = 100, 10 + 10 sweeps, 40 runs per condition, 21,520 runs) and long runs
  (N = 100/400/1600, 200 + 200 sweeps, 16 runs each, 11,520 runs).

## Findings (surrogates, not yet live networks)

1. **The naive Ising reading mispredicts in both directions, and the fitted structure decides which.**
   Neutral framing, ρ = 0.25, N = 100:
   - Qwen-122B (sums, α ≈ 0; inertia γ ≈ 2.2; rivals mildly attract) orders: |m| 0.39 vs 0.09 for its
     Ising reading.
   - Llama-70B (α ≈ 1.4 averaging) and GPT-6-luna stay disordered, like their Ising readings (0.13 vs 0.12;
     0.11 vs 0.10).
   - GPT-6-luna, the most Ising-like endpoint in Stage 1, matches its Ising reading at ρ = 0 too
     (0.16 vs 0.16).
2. **Rival ties often fail to frustrate.** Where rivals are ignored or mildly attract (Gemma, Gemini,
   Qwen-122B), surrogates keep consensus at ρ = 0.25 while the Ising reading is already disordered.
3. **Political framing freezes agents by stubbornness, not frustration.** Persistence ≥ 0.8 with flip rate
   ≤ 0.02 and 0.13–0.33 excess unsatisfied ties above the frustration floor. These frozen states are not
   low-frustration states.
4. **Content framings: most of the "consensus" is the content field; the social part is the amplification
   on top.**
   - Workplace fields-only |m| is 0.92 for Gemma-26B, 0.72 for Mistral Large, 0.54–0.55 for Qwen-122B and
     Nemo.
   - Amplification: Nemo workplace 0.55 → 0.89; Qwen-9B political 0.39 → 0.62.
5. **Directed tie perceptions (ε) and ER vs regular graphs barely matter at Stage 3 scale.**
   - ε changes |m| by ≤ 0.06 for the tested populations, which are already near-disordered at ρ ≥ 0.25,
     except Qwen-122B.
   - α-driven non-reciprocity (ER vs RRG) changes |m| by ≤ 0.16 and is not systematic.
6. **Finite-size scaling (400 sweeps).**
   - In neutral framing, with no net field, spontaneous order persists to N = 1600 for Qwen-122B
     (ρ ≤ 0.25), Gemma-26B (ρ ≤ 0.25), Gemma-31B (ρ = 0) and Gemini (ρ ≤ 0.25).
   - Llama-70B neutral coarsens slowly: the slope CI includes both "order" and slow decay, so it is
     unclear. That is consistent with voter-like dynamics, which do not order as N → ∞.
   - Most low-coupling surrogates follow ⟨m²⟩ ∝ 1/N (no order).
   - In political framing, persistent ⟨m²⟩ includes field-driven alignment and is not a phase transition.
7. **Mixed populations land between or below their components.** Llama-70B + GPT-6-luna churns;
   Llama-70B + Qwen-122B reaches |m| 0.41–0.51 at ρ = 0.

## Bugs found before reporting

- **Ising baseline β.** It initially used the surrogate's k = 1-scale coefficients; fixed to the Stage 1
  additive β. Llama-70B's Ising β had been 6.95 instead of 0.65.
- **Frustration-floor seeds.** Repeats initially reused one seed.
- **Random-regular generator.** Full-rejection pairing failed; replaced with networkx.
